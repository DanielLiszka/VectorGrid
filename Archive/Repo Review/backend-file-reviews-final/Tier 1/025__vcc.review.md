## Consolidated Judgment

The fourth pass improves on the earlier packets and should become the final implementation guide for this file. `vcc.rs` is still a strategy-owner policy surface whose defaults, mapping behavior, CPU/GPU staging helpers, and desired-position state machine are too coupled for a casual structural refactor. But the earlier pure-defer bucket left real value on the table.

The strongest fourth-pass finding is that the most sensitive VCC behavior is lightly pinned in always-on tests. `build_vcc_desired_positions()` still tolerates shape mismatches by degrading into `NaN`-driven no-trade behavior, and the flat-entry confirmation path is easier to misread than its name suggests. Those are good reasons to add guardrails now without changing strategy semantics.

## Safe-Now Priority

- Add deterministic, non-ignored tests for:
  - current flat-entry behavior before any squeeze release
  - `confirm_bars` timing around a release
  - the relationship between `compute_vcc_fast_first_valid()` and `compute_vcc_start_t()`
  - a tiny `vcc_momentum_roundtrip_parity()` fixture
- Add debug-only shape assertions in `build_vcc_desired_positions()` so mismatched signal lengths fail loudly in debug and test builds.
- Add rustdoc where the public traps are:
  - `VccPeriodSweepDevice::first_valid_for_period()` is not the full fast-signal warmup rule
  - route diagnostics record helper fallback events, not full route truth

## Deferred Work

- Do not change `release_confirmed` semantics in a small hardening patch.
- Do not tighten deserialization behavior or introduce `deny_unknown_fields` here without treating it as request-compatibility work.
- Do not extract CPU/CUDA submodules or redesign the owned-device and row-pointer surface without parity review across the VCC companion files.

## Regression And Feature-Preservation Constraints

- Preserve current desired-position semantics unless CPU/GPU parity and validation outcomes are rechecked together.
- Preserve current defaulting and omission-based request behavior unless compatibility is being changed deliberately.
- Treat the owned-device and row-pointer helpers as GPU-companion-facing surfaces even though most of the safe-now work here is CPU-side.

## Final Conclusion

The authoritative implementation-facing conclusion is:

- move `vcc.rs` from pure defer into `Safe-now hardening; larger redesign still deferred`
- keep the current strategy semantics intact
- spend the immediate effort on tests, debug-only invariants, and targeted rustdoc
- defer structural cleanup, serde tightening, and strategy-semantic rewrites until VCC parity is audited end to end
